What it is

The On-Track and College & Career Readiness Dashboard surfaces high-school accountability metrics — cohort, 1st/2nd/3rd-year on-track-to-graduate, and CCR — for district administrators and school leaders. Six panels span district roll-up to individual-student audit.

College vs Career — meet ONE to qualify

College ready

Any one of:
  • ACT ≥ 20
  • SAT ≥ 1040
  • AP Score ≥ 3
  • IB ≥ 4
  • Cambridge ≥ C
  • Dual Enrollment ≥ 6 credit hrs

Career ready

Any one of:
  • CTE Completer status
  • WBL Completer status
  • WIN SC Career Ready ≥ Level 3
  • ASVAB ≥ 31
  • SC HS Employability Credential

Quick steps

Sign in (portal.edanalytics.app)

SCDE/district Microsoft account (SSO).

Open Administrator Navigator

Click the Administrator Navigator tile.

Open the dashboard

Expand Accountability in the left menu → (Pre-Release) On-Track and College & Career Readiness.

Read the headline

5 stat cards: Total Students, College or Career Ready, On-Track, College Ready, Career Ready.

Find what's contributing

Scroll to Components Detail. The longest blue bar = your biggest pathway.

Sort the cohort table

Click % On-Track to surface flagged school × cohort combinations.

Audit a specific student

Student ID filter → name or SUNS ID → Apply Filters → review the Individual Students Perform table → export if needed.

Two facts to remember

One pathway is enough. A student only needs to meet ONE college-ready criterion OR ONE career-ready criterion to be in the CCR numerator.

0% On-Track is a flag, not a verdict. Investigate — often grades aren't finalized, or a small cohort is making the metric volatile.
