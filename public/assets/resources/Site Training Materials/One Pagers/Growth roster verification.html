Growth Roster Verification

Verify student–teacher links, time together, and attribution.
DATA VERIFICATION
v1.1 · June 29, 2026

Two-phase workflow

1 · Verify source dataReview links and dates; correct PowerSchool; publish through Ed-Fi.
2 · Propose attributionAfter verification, update only Proposed % Attributed through the approved secure process.

Use the two tables

Roster TableMain linkage, role, duration, and attribution list.
Course Section EnrollmentsEducator/student begin and end dates for investigation.
% Attributed = Enrollment Duration ÷ Course Duration × 100, capped at 100%. Applicable high-school enrollment duration is capped by the EOCEP end.

Review for

  • One unique Educator ID per educator
  • Complete educator–student links
  • Correct course section and classroom position
  • Reasonable enrollment and course durations
  • Relevant Satchel subject designation
Correct the source. Make linkage and date changes in PowerSchool—not in a download—and publish through Ed-Fi.

Recommended review sequence

  1. Open Administrator Navigator → Data Verification → Growth Roster Verification.
  2. Select year, LEA, school, course, and section; choose Apply filters.
  3. Review the Roster Table.
  4. Cross-filter to Course Section Enrollments for begin/end dates.
  5. Correct PowerSchool and publish.
  6. Confirm the dashboard after its next refresh.

Attribution file rules

  • Download the verified Roster Table, generally as CSV.
  • Change only Proposed % Attributed.
  • Enter 0 to remove an attribution; do not delete the row.
  • Leave every other column unchanged.
  • Use the approved secure-sharing method. Never email roster files.
Before sharing: confirm filters, export date, row count, file scope, and recipient need. Files contain student information.

Troubleshooting clues

  • Missing link: course designation or section assignment
  • Unexpected duration: compare educator and student dates
  • Duplicate growth records: check Educator ID
  • Dashboard mismatch: confirm Ed-Fi publishing and refresh
