What it is

Teacher Navigator's Groups and Interventions feature lets administrators assemble student cohorts beyond classroom/grade rollups, share them with the staff doing the work, and track the interventions and notes that go with each cohort. Purpose-built for MTSS workflows.

Who can do what

Creators

School & district admins
  • Create & edit groups
  • Add/remove students & staff
  • See all groups they administer

Viewers

Teachers, interventionists
  • View shared groups
  • Add notes & interventions
  • Cannot edit group membership

The MTSS pattern

(1) Identify a cohort needing support — DIBELS screening, Well-Being Survey results, attendance flags. (2) Admin creates the group, names it, picks school or district scope. (3) Add the students, add the staff who'll deliver the intervention. (4) On the Updates tab, log the intervention with start/end dates + a description. (5) Use the Assessments tab partway through to see if it's working. (6) Adjust, document in notes, repeat.

Quick steps — Admin role

Sign in (portal.edanalytics.app)

SCDE/district Microsoft account (SSO).

Open Teacher Navigator

Click the Teacher Navigator tile.

Click the group icon

Left sidebar, alongside find a…, grade, class, student.

Click + add group

Name it. Pick school or district scope.

Add students & staff

Two tabs. Filter by name, classroom, grade, school. Check, then add to group.

Click view group

Land on the group's data view. Four tabs: at a glance, updates, assessments, well-being.

Open Updates → log an intervention

+ Add New under interventions. Name, description, content area, start & end dates.

FERPA caution

Adding a staff member to a group gives them student-level access to the students in it. Granting school/district admin rights gives them access to every student in that scope — not just the group. Use the admin-creates / interventionist-views pattern unless full admin is appropriate.

Caveats

  • One-day data lag. When you add or remove a student, their data updates in the group's tabs the next day. They appear in the ADDED/REMOVED sidepanel in the interim.
  • Admins only can edit. Teachers and interventionists can view + add notes/interventions; they can't change group membership.
  • Staff must be in Ed-Fi. If a staff member doesn't appear in the staff list, they need a school association in Ed-Fi or admin rights in the EA User Management application.
  • Remote/program schools may be limited. Students at remote schools or special programs may not appear in the available-students list — EA is working on this.
  • SC Ready score filter doesn't show year. Verify against your SIS for high-stakes group composition until the year is added to the UI.

Two things to remember

Updates is the MTSS tab. The other three tabs (at a glance, assessments, well-being) you already know from class/grade views. Updates is what makes Groups useful for tracked intervention work.

Standardize group names. "Tier 2 Reading · Grade 3 · Fall 25-26" scales; "Reading group" doesn't.
