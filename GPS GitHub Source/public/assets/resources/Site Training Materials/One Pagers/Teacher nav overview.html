What it is

Teacher Navigator is the GPS ecosystem's classroom-facing view of student attendance, assessments, behavior, and well-being. It organizes everything into three nested levels so you can start with a whole grade and drill down to a single student.

The three levels

GradeWidest

A whole grade at a school. Spot cohort-wide patterns.

ClassroomNarrower

One course section. Compare your class to the district average.

StudentDeepest

One child's full picture, including programs & CCR.

Tabs you'll see

at a glance
Headline stats
assessments
Test results
performance over time
Student only

Plus well-being, behavior, and attendance depending on the level. Learn one level and the others feel familiar.

How to read the bands

Assessment results show a colored proficiency bar — red/orange (lower) through green/blue (higher). The filled segment is where the group falls. Expand any result to see it broken out by subscore (skill area).

Quick start

Sign in (portal.edanalytics.app)

Use your SCDE/district account.

Click GPS | Teacher Navigator

You open to your grade levels.

Use the left rail to move

find a… (search) · grade · class · student · group · tools.

Open a level & pick a tab

at a glance, assessments, well-being, behavior, etc.

The drill-down move

(1) Grade → is there a pattern across the cohort? (2) Classroom → compare this class vs. district average. (3) Student → check subscores for which skills and performance over time for the trend. (4) Act: subscores say what to reteach; the trend says how urgent.

Good to know

"Powered by Rally." Teacher Navigator is the SCDE name for EA's "Rally." Seeing the Rally logo in the app is expected.

Behavior tab is empty for SC. South Carolina doesn't feed behavior data here, so the behavior tab and "behavior incidents" stat won't populate — ignore them.

What you see = your access. Missing a grade, class, or student is usually an access/roster question — route it to your school's GPS contact.

Two things to remember

Go wide to narrow. Grade finds the pattern, Classroom locates it, Student names who and what.

State equivalent puts different tests on one yardstick — that's the scale the performance over time chart uses.
