What it is

The ADM Dashboard surfaces your district's Education Finance Act (EFA) and Add-on Weights (AOW) counts using the rules that drive South Carolina school funding. It tells you, at each standard reporting day, how many funded students your district will count — and how that breaks down by EFA code, school, grade, and student.

EFA vs Add-on Weights

EFA

Base per-pupil count
Every enrolled student counted once. Drives the per-pupil base allocation.

AOW

Funded subgroups, weighted
Layered on top of EFA. Poverty, EL, students with disabilities, etc. Drives supplementary funding.

How to use it — by lens

District lens

Verify EFA and AOW counts before funding cycles lock in. Drill from district totals to schools to students to find anomalies. Export Day-45 snapshots for finance and audit.

Principal lens

Filter the School Report to your building. Confirm AOW flags are right (poverty, EL, gifted) — those drive the funding your school receives.

Quick steps

Sign in (portal.edanalytics.app)

SCDE/district Microsoft account (SSO).

Open Administrator Navigator

Click the Administrator Navigator tile.

Select ADM

Top-level item in the left nav: Average Daily Membership (ADM).

Set required filters

School Year → LEA Name → Day of School Year (try 45) → Apply filters.

Read the four stat cards

EFA Total Days, EFA ADM, AOW Total Days, AOW ADM — your headline numbers.

Drill down the panel stack

District → School → Student to find the source of any anomaly.

Export the Day-45 snapshot

⋮ on any panel → CSV or Excel. Save it — data doesn't freeze.

Most important caveat

ADM data does NOT freeze after reporting days pass. If you need a point-in-time value for finance or audit, export the CSV/Excel on the reporting day and save it as your authoritative record. The dashboard is not your archive — your export is.

Other caveats

  • Year-format inconsistency. The Year filter may show single-year ("2026") vs other dashboards' "25-26" format. EA is standardizing.
  • No 180-day filter yet. Useful for financial audits — under consideration based on user feedback.
  • ADM ≠ raw enrollment. ADM averages student-days across a reporting period. A single-day PowerSchool roster pull won't match.
  • EFA Total Days ≤ PowerSchool enrollment. EFA applies specific inclusion / exclusion rules. If EFA shows more than PowerSchool, submit a ticket.

Two facts worth memorizing

The dashboard knows your specific 45th day. No need to translate calendar dates — pick the day number, it handles your district's calendar.

EFA and AOW are parallel calculations. EFA = base count, AOW = funded subgroups weighted on top. The four stat cards show both totals and both averages side by side.
