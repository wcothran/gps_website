Current-year accountability metrics for SC district & school leaders.
v1.1 · 2026-05-14
For: District accountability staff & data coordinators Principals & school leadership teams SCDE program-office analysts

What it is

The Metrics Dashboard is the front door of Administrator Navigator for School Report Card data. It surfaces most of the metrics from the SC School Report Cards using current-year data — before the report card cycle locks in. Built for district accountability staff who need visibility at any point in the year.

How to use it — by lens

District lens

Move from LEA/District name → school distribution → cross-school comparison. Use exports for board-ready charts and to brief building leaders with consistent data.

Principal lens

Start in School View. Compare your subgroup splits to the district. Use Previous Year Value to see whether your gap is widening or closing — and to anchor MTSS conversations.

What metrics and KPIs to look out for

Profiency Percentages

Shows the % of students that pass certain exam thresholds, proficiency scores, and other relevant metrics.

Quick steps

Sign in (portal.edanalytics.app)

Use your SCDE/district account (SSO).

Open Administrator Navigator

Click the Administrator Navigator tile in the app launcher.

Select the dashboard

In the left menu, expand Accountability and select Metrics Dashboard.

Set filters & apply

School Year → Metric Category → District/School Level Metric → Apply Filters.

Move through the three views

District → School → Student to zoom from rollup to roster.

Export when you need to share

⋮ (top-right of any panel) → CSV or Excel.

Important caveats

  • Not 1:1 with the report card. Historical non Ed-Fi data and manual SCDE corrections can cause numbers to differ from official report card values.
  • Thresholds are not annualized. The current year's rules (e.g., SAT 1040 for 2025-26) are applied retroactively to every year shown.
  • School Environment metrics differ from standalone dashboards. Chronic Absenteeism and ADA use accountability-lens inclusion rules here; numbers will diverge slightly from the dedicated Chronic Absenteeism / ADA dashboards by design.
  • On-Track uses earned credit only. In-progress credits are excluded — expect mid-year values to look lower until second-semester credits are finalized.
  • Not all data are available all year. Categories light up at different points as data arrives (e.g., SAT scores don't land until July at the earliest). Student Climate Survey is not yet included.

One worked example to anchor on

Pick a metric → set Subgroup Category = Birth Sex → look at the gap in District View → drop to School View to find which schools drive the gap → drop to Student View to investigate. The same District → School → Student funnel works for every subgroup the dashboard supports.

Two facts worth memorizing

PowerSchool correction → dashboard the next day. Monday fix → Tuesday visible. That changes data-quality work from a quarterly project to a weekly habit.

"NA" usually means suppression. Cell counts too small to display under student-privacy rules. Submit a ticket if it shows up unexpectedly.
