What this dashboard does

Shows how PowerSchool and Ed-Fi student records align with established cohort-maintenance rules used for Graduation Rate, CCR, and HSSSI denominators.

It does not redefine the cohort. It is a visibility and verification tool. Source corrections happen in PowerSchool.

Core 9GR rules

  • 9GR identifies the spring year of a student’s first U.S. high-school year.
  • A blank 9GR excludes the student; the model does not derive one.
  • Valid records generally need 9GR and enrollment in the same year.
  • Include in Denominator = No excludes the student under applicable rules.
  • Entry and exit before day 45 do not create an automatic model exclusion.

Three common reviews

  1. Students in a cohort: filter 9GR plus district or school In Cohort.
  2. Students not in a cohort: read Reason Not Included and both inclusion columns.
  3. Students without 9GR: review school, grade, day-45, entry/exit, and exit code.

Why district and school may differ

Specified residential treatment, group-home, adjudication, and non-high-school enrollment statuses can retain district responsibility while removing school attribution.

Correction sequence

  1. Identify the field driving the status.
  2. Compare the dashboard with PowerSchool and documented history.
  3. Make the approved PowerSchool correction.
  4. Publish through Ed-Fi.
  5. Confirm the result after the next dashboard refresh.
Cross-district logic: if multiple districts have valid records in one year, the latest enrollment determines the retained record.
Use current cycle guidance. Follow the active accountability deadlines, correction window, and support process; dates are intentionally omitted here.
