Growth: Student Progress

Two models. One report-card indicator. Different questions.
GRADES 4–8
v1.1 · June 29, 2026

Indicator

Student Progress is one of eight rated report-card components and has 35 possible points for elementary and middle schools.

Two growth models

Value-Added Model (VAM)How far did actual average growth exceed or fall short of predicted growth?
Added-Value Growth Model (AVGM)How fully did individual growth records meet or exceed MAT and AVT targets?

AVGM targets

  • MAT: 50th-percentile expected growth.
  • AVT: 55th–80th-percentile target, higher for lower-achieving students.
  • Produces rating points from percentages and from target attainment.
Prediction ≠ target ≠ proficiency. Keep these three comparison points separate.

2024–2025 score structure

Math

Targets + percentages + Value-Added rating points

ELA

Value-Added rating points only because of scale changes

VAM includes All Students and Lowest Quintile components.

Choose the right view

  • Growth Dashboard: comparisons and visuals
  • Metrics Dashboard: broad metric tables and denominators
  • Spreadsheet: authorized student-level target records

Interpret responsibly

  • Name the model and component.
  • State subject, grade, school level, and year.
  • Include the denominator.
  • Do not equate missing a target with no growth.
  • Check the current manual before reusing a prior-year formula.
